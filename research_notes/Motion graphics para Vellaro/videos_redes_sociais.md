# Motion graphics for short-form social video (Reels, Stories, TikTok, Shorts, paid social): luxury custom furniture / joinery, applied to Vellaro Design

Research notes, compiled 2026-10-31. Research method note: web search only. Direct page fetches were blocked in this session for `ads.tiktok.com` and `www.hopperhq.com` (network allowlist), so TikTok's and Meta's official pages could not be read in full. Many figures below come from search-result summaries of secondary blogs that cite the platforms. Each one is flagged where the primary source was not verified. Vendor and agency blogs dominate this topic, and they are treated as heuristics, not evidence.

## 1. Which motion graphics styles and formats perform for luxury furniture/interior brands (with named brand examples)?

### Takeaway
I found no published performance data that compares motion graphics formats for luxury furniture specifically. The evidence is indirect. Large Italian houses (Minotti, Molteni&C) lean on cinematic, product-centred storytelling and close-up detail rather than loud kinetic templates. CGI-led formats, including product configuration videos and "fake out-of-home" (FOOH), are the documented route to virality for furniture. For a small workshop, the feasible core is real process and macro footage with restrained typographic overlays. CGI and "drawing to product" reveals can serve as occasional hero pieces.

### Cited Findings
- **Minotti, Sept 2025 ad campaign:** four visuals of its 2025 seating systems ran in architecture, design and lifestyle publications in more than 50 countries. The brand describes it as "a new phase" in its storytelling that puts the product itself at the centre of the narrative, with close-up compositions of bespoke detailing. This was a print/publication campaign, not video. — [Minotti, "On air the new ADV campaign 2025"](https://www.minotti.com/en/on-air-the-new-adv-campaign-2025_3)
- **Minotti, "The Grand Tour" (2023, older):** a 75th-anniversary film that presents the Minotti lifestyle through animated cinematic scenarios across five locations. It shows a top-tier brand using CGI/animated cinematic scenes rather than live-action shoots. — [ArchDaily](https://www.archdaily.com/1003442/from-sketch-to-render-how-to-integrate-artificial-intelligence-into-the-workflow); [designboom](https://www.designboom.com/design/architonic-minotti-lifestyle-the-grand-tour-film-07-05-2023/); [Design Anthology](https://design-anthology.com/story/minotti-presents-the-grand-tour)
- **Minotti, Salone 2025:** the presentation addressed both longtime clients and younger buyers, emphasising cosy spaces, "embracing curves" and careful material selection. — [LUXUO](https://www.luxuo.com/properties/interiors/must-see-highlights-from-salone-del-mobile-2025.html)
- **Molteni&C:** the 90th anniversary was marked with a "cinematic" book ("Molteni Mondo"), and the brand story is framed around craftsmanship and sustainability. I found no specific 2025 short-form video campaign. — [Galerie Magazine](https://galeriemagazine.com/molteni-90th-anniversary/)
- **B&B Italia, Poliform, RH:** search turned up no documented 2025–26 social video or motion campaign (B&B Italia coverage was limited to Salone 2025 products, such as the Tufty-Time anniversary). — [LUXUO](https://www.luxuo.com/properties/interiors/must-see-highlights-from-salone-del-mobile-2025.html)
- **Ornare (Brazil):** I found no documented 2025 video campaign. Brand context from 2025:
  - It signs high-end residential developments, including **ORO Ilha Pura in Rio de Janeiro** and Next Itaim in São Paulo. Executives estimate the Ornare signature raises the developments' VGV (gross sales value) by about 30%. — [Bloomberg Línea](https://www.bloomberglinea.com.br/negocios/ornare-acelera-expansao-com-projetos-assinados-em-novos-edificios-de-alto-padrao/)
  - It marked its 40th anniversary at Casa Cor BH 2025, with 17 showrooms in Brazil and 14 abroad. — [Hoje em Dia](https://www.hojeemdia.com.br/genteenegocios/ornare-celebra-40-anos-com-presenca-na-casa-cor-bh-1.1081042)
  - Its own 2025 preview framed the year as "design from a new perspective", uniting art and fashion with design. — [Ornare Preview 2025](https://www.ornare.com.br/?p=20314); [Ornare 2025 Retrospective](https://www.ornare.com.br/?p=22131)
- **Breton, Todeschini, Florense:** a Portuguese-language search found no coverage of their Reels or motion strategy.
- **CGI furniture campaigns:**
  - **DE.CI:** a CGI-led campaign placed its furniture in large public environments, turning them into "digital showrooms", and shared the result on TikTok and Instagram. The source is a vendor blog, and its virality claims are unverified. — [VividWorks, "Best furniture marketing campaigns (2025)"](https://www.vividworks.com/blog/best-furniture-marketing-campaigns)
  - **FOOH as a format:** Jacquemus's 2023 oversized handbags on Paris streets went viral across TikTok, Instagram and LinkedIn. Audiences split between those who thought it was a real stunt and those who spotted the CGI. — [Ellie Motion](https://elliemotion.com/journal/fake-out-of-home-campaigns-go-viral-the-anatomy-of-shareable-cgi-moments)
  - **Configurator-style 3D video:** the Palliser case study describes a 3D video showing multiple upholstered items and several configurations of the same product in one interior scene. This is an agency portfolio page, not performance data. — [CGIFurniture, Palliser case study](https://cgifurniture.com/case-study/upholstered-furniture-3d-visualization-for-palliser/)
- **Furniture content mix:** a 2026 furniture-marketing guide calls Instagram the highest-converting social platform for furniture brands. It recommends room-scene imagery as roughly 40% of posts and 3–4 Reels per week. This is a vendor blog with unsourced figures. — [FurnCMO](https://furncmo.com/blog/furniture-social-media-strategy)

### Inferences
- High-end benchmarks (Minotti 2025) point toward **product-at-centre, close-up detail, slow cinematic movement**, not trend-template edits. For Vellaro, macro texture shots (veneer grain, brass hardware, LED edge light catching a lacquer surface) with one or two serif text lines fit both the benchmark and the brand palette.
- **Process / making-of** footage (CNC, hand finishing, assembly, installation) is the most authentic and cheapest format for a workshop that already produces these moments. It also differentiates "sob medida" from catalogue furniture. Brand examples for this format were not documented in the sources found, so it is an inference.
- The **"drawing / 3D model → finished piece" reveal** (SketchUp/Promob render or a line drawing dissolving into a real shot from the same angle) fits a bespoke business naturally, because every project already has a 3D design. It doubles as proof that "what you approve is what you get". It needs only a locked-off camera matched to the render angle and a cross-dissolve or wipe mask in CapCut/After Effects.
- **Before/after (empty room → installed joinery)** is a strong fit for the Rio apartment market, where Ornare signs developments such as Ilha Pura. Brazilian high-end clients are clearly receptive to "signed" joinery.
- Full CGI and FOOH are viral-capable but costly. For a small studio they are an occasional hero piece, not a weekly format.

### Gaps
- No quantitative comparison of format performance (process vs. CGI vs. before/after) for furniture or luxury interiors exists in the sources found.
- No verifiable data on Minotti, Poliform, Molteni, B&B Italia, RH, Ornare, Breton, Todeschini or Florense Instagram Reels engagement. That would need manual review of their accounts or a social analytics tool, which was outside this session's reach.
- Stop-motion for furniture was not researched (no relevant results).

## 2. Hooks, ideal length, pacing, captions/on-screen text, sound and loops (2025–2026 evidence)

### Takeaway
Platform guidance converges on three points: state the proposition in the first ~3 seconds, design for sound-on while captioning for sound-off, and keep text readable (TikTok suggests 5–10 words per second when text is used). Distribution on Instagram is driven by watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach. Most evidence favours roughly 15–60 s pieces for ads and up to ~90 s for organic Reels, but the data is survey-based and inconsistent.

### Cited Findings
- **TikTok official guidance** (Creative best practices for performance ads, reported as last updated June 2025):
  - Introduce the content proposition in the first 3 seconds for better recall and awareness.
  - Prioritise the hook in the first 6 seconds to boost engagement and watch time, using suspense, surprise or other emotions.
  - When using text, display 5–10 words per second.
  - Source: [TikTok Ads Help Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en). The page could not be fetched directly; these points come from search snippets.
- **Third-party "3-second rule":** sources claim TikTok uses 3-second view rate as an early ad-quality signal. TikTok's own page does not confirm this, so it is unverified. — [Stackmatix](https://stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-creative-best-practices-2026); [Opus Clip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/tiktok-hook-formulas)
- **Instagram ranking signals:** Adam Mosseri named three signals in January 2025: watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach (DM shares).
  - Widely repeated claim: sends weigh "3–5x" likes. I found no primary source for it.
  - A more direct Mosseri quote (via Social Media Today) says sends are only "slightly more important" for reaching unconnected audiences.
  - Source: [Creatorflow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-algorithm/); [PostEverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/how-the-instagram-algorithm-works)
- **Reels length:** Reels of up to 3 minutes became eligible for recommendation to non-followers in 2025. Sources conflict on the date (January vs. August 2025). — [Creatorflow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-algorithm/); [influencers-gone-wild](https://influencers-gone-wild.it.com/?p=343)
- **Originality:** one source claims original, platform-native content gets 40–60% more distribution. The figure comes from a third-party dataset, not Instagram. — [ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/instagram-influencer-marketing/)
- **Length surveys:**
  - Wyzowl 2026 is cited as finding that 71% say videos of 30 s to 2 min are most effective. Secondary sources disagree on whether the respondents were consumers or marketers, and I did not read the primary report. The same report is cited as finding that 91% of businesses use video. — [Pictory](https://pictory.ai/blog/video-marketing-statistics-2026); [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/video-marketing-statistic)
  - HubSpot-attributed: 51% say 30–60 s is the optimal marketing-video length. — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/video-marketing-statistic)
  - Socialinsider 2025 (via ShortsIntel): 60–90 s Reels show the highest engagement rates. — [ShortsIntel](https://www.shortsintel.com/statistics/short-form-video-marketing)
  - Bynder (older, 2024 data): average video length fell from 168 s (2016) to 76 s (2023). — [Siege Media](https://www.siegemedia.com/?p=31239)
- **Sound (Meta Reels ads):**
  - Reels ads default to sound on.
  - A Meta-attributed claim, seen in one secondary source only: 9:16 Reels ads with audio and key messages inside the safe zone had a 34.5% lower cost per result than image ads. Unverified.
  - Captions or text overlays are recommended as a fallback for silent viewing.
  - Source: [WebsiteBuilderExpert, summarising Meta's Reels ads guide](https://www.websitebuilderexpert.com/news/meta-updates-reels-ads-guide/); [Adsmurai](https://www.adsmurai.com/en/articles/meta-reels-ads)
- **Shorts ads:** they can run up to 3 minutes, but only the first 60 s play in the Shorts feed, so Google recommends Shorts ads under 60 s. Sources also say completion / watch-through rate is the main distribution signal for organic Shorts. — [Google Ads Help, "Your guide to YouTube Shorts ads"](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16041697?hl=en); [OpenClip](https://openclip.app/guides/youtube-shorts-length)

### Inferences
- **Recommended lengths for Vellaro:**
  - Paid ads: 9–20 s, with the product or the transformation visible by second 1–3.
  - Organic Reels: 15–45 s for process and reveal pieces, and up to 60–90 s for a full "project story" (drawing → workshop → installation → client space).
  - Each piece should loop cleanly: the last frame matches the first (for example, a closed door at the start and the end), which supports watch time.
- **Hook ideas that stay on-brand** (no shouting text):
  - (a) Open on the finished space with the LED switching on in darkness, the near-black palette making a natural "light reveal".
  - (b) Open mid-action: a blade, a veneer sheet or a hand pressing a push-latch drawer.
  - (c) A single-line serif question or claim, e.g. "Cada milímetro foi desenhado para este apartamento."
- **"Sends" are the scarce signal.** Content that architects, interior designers and couples renovating want to forward to each other (a clever hidden bar, a closet layout, a material swatch comparison) is the target, because Vellaro's WhatsApp-driven sales also begin with a forward.
- **Text:** TikTok's 5–10 words per second is a performance-ad ceiling. A luxury register should sit at the low end or below, with short phrases held on screen at least ~1.5–2 s. This is an inference; no source specifies luxury pacing.
- **Sound:**
  - Design for sound-on, with ASMR-like workshop audio (planing, a soft-close drawer, the click of a magnetic latch) layered under a calm instrumental track or licensed library music.
  - Always burn in or auto-generate captions for any voice-over.
  - Voice-over (the founder or craftsman, in Portuguese) adds authenticity, but no source compared VO vs. music performance for this category.

### Gaps
- No credible 2025–26 study comparing voiceover vs. music-only performance, or measuring loop effects, was found.
- Meta's and TikTok's primary pages could not be fetched; exact current wording is unverified.
- No luxury-specific data on hook styles or pacing.

## 3. Technical specs: aspect ratios, safe zones, resolution, frame rate, file size, text legibility

### Takeaway
Produce masters at **1080×1920 (9:16)**, keep all text and logos inside a central safe area that clears roughly the top 14% (about 250–270 px) and the bottom 35% (about 650–680 px for ads; organic Reels need at least about 350–400 px), with about 6% side margins. Export H.264 MP4 at 30 fps (or 24/25 for a cinematic feel). Also produce a 4:5 cut for Feed placements. Exact pixel values vary across secondary sources, so the Ads Manager safe-zone preview should be the final check.

### Cited Findings
- **Canvas:** 9:16 at 1080×1920 is the standard for Reels, Stories and Shorts. Meta recommends 9:16 for Stories, Status and Reels because most people hold their phones vertically. — [Hopper HQ (2026)](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/); [Adobe Blog (Nov 2024)](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2024/11/04/unlock-potential-reels-best-practices-for-creative-success-using-adobe-creative-cloud); [Knowlify](https://knowlify.com/articles/meta-video-ad-specs)
- **Meta safe zone (secondary sources):**
  - About 14% top, 35% bottom and 6% sides clear across Stories, Reels and in-stream Reels.
  - If the ad has a disclaimer, Meta asks for the bottom 40% to be kept clear.
  - Ads Manager reportedly offers a safe-zone overlay.
  - Source: [House of Marketers](https://houseofmarketers.com/guide-to-safe-zones-tiktok-facebook-instagram-stories-reels/); [Coinis](https://coinis.com/blog/vertical-video-ad-safe-zone-ui-overlay-fix-coinis); [Benly](https://benly.ai/learn/ad-creative/meta-ads-creative-specs-2026)
- **Organic Reels safe-zone estimates conflict:**
  - Top: about 250 px.
  - Bottom: anywhere from 340 px to 380 px.
  - Central "safe box": proposals range from 895×980 to 880×1420 px.
  - One source claims Instagram enlarged the audio/description overlay in late 2025, but this is uncorroborated.
  - Source: [Hopper HQ](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/); [Quso](https://quso.ai/blog/instagram-reel-dimensions); [Blitzcut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-size-instagram-reels-2026)
- **Two versions:** build at least a 9:16 and a 4:5 version of each ad so placements don't crop or cover key content. — [Benly](https://benly.ai/learn/ad-creative/meta-ads-creative-specs-2026); [Adsmurai](https://www.adsmurai.com/en/articles/meta-reels-ads)
- **YouTube Shorts:**
  - Length: up to 3 minutes since 15 Oct 2024, with no minimum.
  - Recommended format: 9:16 at 1080×1920. Sources conflict on whether square 1:1 uploads also count as Shorts.
  - Other recommendations: MP4/MOV, 30 fps, under 100 MB. These come from third-party sources; I did not verify them against YouTube's documentation.
  - Shorts have no end screens or info cards.
  - Source: [Kapwing](https://kapwing.com/resources/youtube-shorts-is-making-a-huge-change-to-its-maximum-video-length-and-it-will-affect-creators); [OpenClip](https://openclip.app/guides/youtube-shorts-length); [ShortSync (2026)](https://shortsync.app/resources/youtube-shorts-upload-requirements-2026); [Satura](https://saturaai.com/blog/youtube-shorts-video-size)
- **TikTok:** the official Creative best-practices page covers the format, but I could not fetch it to confirm safe-zone pixel values. — [TikTok Ads Help Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en)

### Inferences
- **One master, then crops:**
  - Shoot or render at 4K or 1080 vertical (or shoot 4K horizontal and reframe).
  - Export: 1080×1920 for Reels, Stories, TikTok and Shorts; 1080×1350 (4:5) for Feed ads; and check the 1:1/3:4 profile-grid crop for the cover.
- **Text placement:** keep text in the central band, roughly y = 270–1250 px on a 1920 px canvas. Put the wordmark small, top-centre or just above the lower UI zone, never in the bottom ~35%.
- **Legibility on a near-black background:**
  - Silver/off-white serif display at 60–90 px cap size for headlines.
  - A sans or light serif at 40–48 px for supporting lines.
  - Avoid hairline serifs below that size, because they shimmer after Instagram's compression.
  - These figures are inferences from common practice; no primary source with exact sizes was found.
- **Dark footage and compression:**
  - Near-black footage is prone to banding and macro-blocking after compression.
  - Lift shadows slightly (deep charcoal, not pure 0,0,0).
  - Add very light grain.
  - Export at a high bitrate (about 15–20 Mbps for 1080p).
  - This is practitioner inference, not sourced.

### Gaps
- No primary Meta, TikTok or YouTube pages were readable in-session. Exact current safe-zone pixel values and file-size limits remain secondhand.
- No source gave verified minimum text sizes for mobile legibility.

## 4. How a luxury brand keeps motion design premium rather than "templated"

### Takeaway
There is no performance study on this. Practitioner and brand evidence favours restraint: one animated element per screen, consistent slow easing, generous negative space, a serif-led hierarchy and the product as the hero. That matches how Minotti framed its 2025 campaign.

### Cited Findings
- Minotti's 2025 campaign puts "the product itself at the centre of the narrative", with close-ups of bespoke detailing. — [Minotti](https://www.minotti.com/en/on-air-the-new-adv-campaign-2025_3)
- A 2025 design-trends guide recommends one animated heading per screen and consistent easing, so motion feels deliberate. — [Piktochart](https://piktochart.com/blog/graphic-design-trends-2025/)
- Serif typefaces are returning after years of sans-serif dominance, with brands using their "heritage-driven" personality. This is a general trend claim, not luxury-specific. — [Todaymade](https://todaymade.com/blog/typography-trends)
- A motion studio argues that luxury brands favour "smooth, controlled movements that suggest sophistication", while startups choose more energetic motion. This is the studio's own marketing opinion. — [FEVR](https://wearefevr.com/kinetic-typography)
- A kinetic-type roundup contrasts Apple's restraint with Spotify's loud statements, noting that restraint "protects the idea". — [Moonb](https://www.moonb.io/blog/kinetic-typography)

### Inferences (practical rules for Vellaro)
- **Motion vocabulary:**
  - Use only a few moves: slow fades (12–20 frames), soft masked wipes that follow a real edge in the footage (a panel line, a door reveal), gentle letter-spacing tracking-in on the serif wordmark, and slow push-ins (2–5% scale over the shot).
  - Avoid bounces, elastic overshoot, emoji, sticker graphics, auto-caption "word pop" styles, trending template transitions and whip pans.
- **Timing:**
  - Hold shots for 1.5–3 s rather than cutting every 0.5 s, cutting on motion or sound (a drawer closing, a light switching on).
  - The hook can be fast, but the body should breathe.
- **Typography:**
  - One serif display face for headlines, plus at most one quiet sans for specs or captions.
  - Sentence case, wide tracking on small caps labels, and no more than about 6–8 words on screen at once.
- **Color:**
  - Near-black (charcoal) ground, silver/off-white type, and a single warm brass accent used only for emphasis: a thin rule, an underline or an LED glow.
  - Grade footage warm-neutral so wood and brass read rich against the dark ground.
- **Branding:**
  - Use a short (≤1.5 s) consistent end card: the silver wordmark appears through a light sweep (mirroring the LED accent), plus "Orçamento via WhatsApp" or the handle.
  - Using the same end card on every video builds recognition without a templated feel.
- **"Real" over "rendered":** the workshop's real hands, tools and sawdust are the premium signal for bespoke joinery. Motion graphics should annotate (dimensions drawn on, material names, "feito em Rio de Janeiro"), not decorate.
- **Annotated blueprint overlays:**
  - Thin silver line drawings and dimension callouts that trace over real footage, then fade.
  - They translate "sob medida" visually and are achievable in CapCut or After Effects with masks and trim paths.
- **Templates are still useful internally.** Build a small house set (title card, lower third, end card, caption style) once, then reuse it. Consistency is what reads premium; generic marketplace templates are what reads cheap.

### Gaps
- No empirical study comparing restrained vs. high-energy motion performance for luxury categories was found.
- No design-press (It's Nice That, Motionographer, Dezeen, Wallpaper*) article on luxury furniture social motion surfaced in search.

## 5. Brazilian market specifics: Instagram/WhatsApp behaviour for home/furniture purchases

### Takeaway
WhatsApp is the dominant conversational and commercial channel in Brazil, and Instagram is the main discovery and influence platform. There is no published data specific to bespoke or high-end furniture. For Vellaro, the logical funnel is Reels/ads for discovery → click-to-WhatsApp for consultation and quotes, with videos built to be forwarded in WhatsApp.

### Cited Findings
- **WhatsApp on the home screen:** Mobile Time/Opinion Box "Super Panorama" (June 2026) finds WhatsApp on the home screen of about 65% of Brazilian smartphones, up from 53% a year earlier. — [Mobile Time](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/09/06/2026/whatsapp-super-panorama/); [Mobile Time, Super Panorama June 2026](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/17/06/2026/super-panorama-26/)
- **Talking to and buying from brands:**
  - Mobile Time/Opinion Box messaging panorama (edition date unclear, possibly 2024): 74% of monthly active WhatsApp users in Brazil are interested in talking to brands on the platform, and 66% of those want to buy products or services through it.
  - The most common business use of messengers is asking brands questions and requesting information.
  - Source: [Infobip summary of Panorama Mensageria](https://www.infobip.com/pt/blog/panorama-mensageria-brasil-mobile-time-opinion-box); [Panorama Mensageria Mar/2024 PDF (older)](https://static.poder360.com.br/2024/03/Panorama-Mensageria-MAR-24.pdf)
- **Buying through WhatsApp:**
  - CX Trends 2025 (Octadesk + Opinion Box): about 30% of Brazilian consumers already use WhatsApp to make purchases. — [Consumidor Moderno](https://consumidormoderno.com.br/comunicacao-empresas-no-whatsapp-pesquisa/)
  - 76% of Brazilians interact with brands via WhatsApp (Infobip-sponsored survey, date unclear). — [ABC da Comunicação](https://www.abcdacomunicacao.com.br/76-dos-brasileiros-interagem-com-marcas-via-whatsapp-mostra-pesquisa-patrocinada-pela-infobip/)

  - 69% of Brazilian consumers have bought after an influencer recommendation (Opinion Box + Influency.me). — [Meio & Mensagem](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/marketing/brasileiros-confiam-mais-em-preco-do-que-em-influenciadores)

  - PiniOn (July 2025): 50.9% say in-store ambience/decoration is the main purchase stimulus, ahead of ads. — [Mercado & Consumo](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/28/07/2025/noticias-varejo/decoracao-e-experiencia-sao-principal-estimulo-a-compra-para-mais-da-metade-dos-brasileiros/)

### Inferences
- **Every video should have a WhatsApp next step.** Use click-to-WhatsApp ads on Meta, a link sticker in Stories and a "Fale conosco no WhatsApp" end card. Keep the WhatsApp line clean of on-screen clutter, since it sits outside the bottom safe zone in the caption or button.
- **Build "forwardable" assets.** Brazilian buyers often decide jointly (couples, family, the architect), so a 20–30 s Reel that can be forwarded in a WhatsApp chat works as a sales tool. Vellaro can also send these videos directly in quote conversations (a portfolio clip per room type: closet, kitchen, home office, living-room panel).
